IGDA community starting points

Two local starting points for developer meetups and shared practice. Each chapter announces its own events; check its organiser for current details.

Melbourne · VIC

IGDA Melbourne supports knowledge sharing, developer connections and community events, including Megadev. Follow the chapter’s latest announcements.

Its general conditions require attendees to be over 18 and show matching photo ID, unless an event specifies otherwise. Read the entry conditions.

Sydney · NSW

IGDA Sydney’s Beer and Pixels festival meetup includes developer networking and prototype playtesting. Use the dated event announcement for times, venue and entry details.

Making your first visit useful

Check the format

A talk, mixer and playtest are different experiences. Read the organiser’s description and booking requirements.

Ask before bringing gear

Check whether prototypes can be shown and whether space or equipment is provided.

Start a conversation

Prepare a short description of what you are making or what you would like to learn.
