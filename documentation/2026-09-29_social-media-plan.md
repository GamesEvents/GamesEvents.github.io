Lead with: **The Australian GameDev Events calendar is still actively maintained, and we've just added a fresh batch of MIGW and Sydney Games Festival events.**

Speak to Australian game makers, students, people entering the industry, and community organisers. Keep the voice friendly and practical, using “we” for the community calendar. Give each post one main action: browse, subscribe, or submit. “Refreshed” refers to calendar content; the website style trials have not been adopted.

Use existing social accounts. The main captions suit Facebook, LinkedIn, Mastodon, or a community announcement; the short versions suit a single short post. For Instagram, use the main caption with “Calendar link in bio” in place of the URL after adding the calendar website to the profile. A Story can use a link sticker. Community group/Discord sharing should follow the group's promotion rules.
