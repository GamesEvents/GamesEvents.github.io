# Archived 2026 festival imports

Prepared on 29 September 2026: **24 events** for Melbourne International Games Week and Sydney Games Festival. Comparison used the 26 September full-calendar export, which is no longer present in the checkout. David subsequently moved all 25 generated files (24 individual files plus their combined batch) into `ics/archive/` as old/already-uploaded history. **Status: archived by the Operator; excluded from the pending queue.**

The archived [combined batch](../../ics/archive/2026-festival-new-events.ics) and individual files below contain the same event UIDs. They are historical references, not a new upload queue. The combined file contains only this batch, not the entire community calendar.

For new event preparation, start with the [event workflow](../event-workflow.md) and [current import queue](../../ics/README.md). Do not requeue the archived occurrences. Archived event facts reflect the original research date and need rechecking before any future correction.

High Score (3–4 October) and GCAP (5–7 October) are single multi-day all-day entries, as requested. Play Now is a date-only entry on 8 October: its public pages do not specify attendance hours or a venue. Timed events use UTC instants converted from Melbourne/Sydney local time, including the 4 October daylight-saving change. Beer and Pixels is a one-off festival entry because the existing series explicitly excludes its October occurrence.
